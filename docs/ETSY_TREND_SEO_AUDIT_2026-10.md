# Etsy trend, product, and SEO audit

Updated: 2026-10-06

This is a planning reference, not a claim that every saved bestseller or trend will perform for a new shop. Saved Paste MCP material is treated as a lead; Etsy's current Seller Handbook, Marketplace Insights, and live category pages are the validation layer.

## Highest-priority product tests

1. Digital 20oz tumbler-wrap bundles: straight/tapered PNGs, seasonal themes, and original Ichotaku/TrashCat visual systems.
2. Niche graphic T-shirts: nocturnal music, dark humor, broadcast/archive, surreal bird, and alternative-culture themes.
3. Original SVG/PNG bundles: mascot, crow, cassette, radio, skull, and occult-symbol sets without protected characters.
4. Gift-oriented variants: music-fan, cat-owner, gothic, Halloween, and personalized-name concepts where the product actually supports personalization.
5. Printable wall art and digital posters: signalwave, dark academia, surreal blue-bird, and gothic radio themes.

Etsy's live tumbler category currently surfaces large PNG/SVG bundles, 20oz skinny wraps, straight/tapered files, sublimation designs, seasonal files, and add-your-own-photo/name products. The marketplace examples are directional evidence, not guaranteed revenue.

## SEO structure

Use one exact product phrase, one theme/audience phrase, and one format/occasion phrase.

### Tumbler examples

- `Gothic 20oz Tumbler Wrap PNG — Straight and Tapered Sublimation Design`
- `Halloween Radio Tumbler Wrap — 20oz Skinny PNG Digital Download`
- `Music Lover Tumbler PNG — Dark Signalwave Sublimation Wrap`

### T-shirt examples

- `TrashCat Dark Humor Graphic T-Shirt — Alternative Music Lover Shirt`
- `Surreal Blue Bird Graphic Tee — Indie Music Art Shirt`
- `Nocturnal Signalwave Shirt — Gothic Broadcast Graphic Tee`

### Tags and description

Start with product terms: `20oz tumbler wrap`, `tumbler wrap PNG`, `skinny tumbler PNG`, `sublimation design`, `graphic T-shirt`, `digital download`. Add motif, audience, and occasion terms only when the design genuinely matches. Use the description to state format, included files, dimensions, resolution, digital-only status, delivery, and license terms.

## Aspect-ratio production presets

| Asset | Working preset |
|---|---:|
| Etsy hero/listing image | 3000 × 2400 (5:4) |
| Universal listing image | 3000 × 2250 (4:3) |
| Square product tile | 3000 × 3000 (1:1) |
| Shirt front mockup | 3000 × 3000 or 3000 × 3750 |
| Poster/wall art | 4:5 or 2:3 |
| Shop banner | 3360 × 840 (4:1) |
| Existing tumbler video | 2500 × 1736, 10 seconds |

Keep the first listing image landscape or square, with a single readable subject and safe margins for thumbnail cropping. Etsy recommends listing images at least 2000 pixels wide; transparent PNG areas can render black in listing imagery.
