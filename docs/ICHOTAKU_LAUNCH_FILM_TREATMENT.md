# IchoTAKU launch film treatment

## Working title

**THE SIGNAL IS STILL TRANSMITTING**

## Design read

An atmospheric 60-second brand-world film for an anime/digital-art Etsy shop, aimed at curious collectors and makers who want artwork with a story. The visual language is midnight cyberpunk: black space, electric blue guidance, signal red interruption, rain-slick city light, broadcast UI, and the blue bird as a recurring guide.

## Strategy spine

```text
Audience:
  People who collect anime-inspired art, decorate personal spaces,
  make apparel or gifts, and want digital artwork with identity.

They currently believe:
  Digital art shops are interchangeable image folders.

We need them to believe:
  IchoTAKU is a living transmission with a recognizable mythology,
  and each artwork is an artifact from that world.

Single launch promise:
  Find the signal. Carry the story forward.

Proof ladder:
  1. Product proof: real IchoTAKU artwork and product mockups.
  2. World proof: the broadcaster, city, blue bird, signal language,
     and recurring visual system.
  3. Shop proof: actual Etsy listing/product pages and file details,
     shown only after those facts are verified.

Desired feeling:
  Discovery, belonging, midnight curiosity, creative permission.

CTA:
  Enter the signal — ichotaku.etsy.com

Claims requiring approval:
  Product formats, commercial-use rights, file types, pricing,
  availability, sales, reviews, and any “original” or “official” claim.

Things to avoid:
  Fake customer testimonials, invented sales numbers, fake Etsy UI,
  unlicensed anime characters, unapproved logos, and claims that the
  blue bird represents a real person or franchise.
```

## Creative route

### Route: The midnight transmission

A silent receiver flickers on in a rain-soaked city. Static becomes a red waveform. A blue feather crosses the frame. The IchoTAKU broadcaster turns toward the signal, and the shop’s art appears as recovered transmissions: a character, a bird, a radio pulse, a design sheet, a product mockup. The final reveal is not “buy this image”; it is “you found the archive.”

Why it fits:

- It turns the existing brand language into a narrative instead of decorating a product montage.
- It gives the blue bird a meaningful role: guide, memory, and transition device.
- It can support shirts, books, prints, tumblers, and digital downloads without pretending they are one physical product.
- It works muted through title cards and sound-led through radio noise, low synth, and a final broadcast lock.

## Visual system

### Palette

Use the existing local brand behavior rather than introducing a new palette:

- near-black / ink field;
- cold electric blue for guidance, interface traces, and the bird;
- signal red for interruption, emphasis, and the final pulse;
- restrained off-white for readable type;
- occasional moon-red as a scene light, not a full-screen gradient.

### Composition

- Keep the main subject slightly off-center, as if observed through a broadcast frame.
- Use horizontal scanlines, edge telemetry, small frequency labels, and frame counters sparingly.
- Let the bird cross between scenes to create continuity.
- Use hard vertical panels for archive/product reveals; use wide cinematic frames for the city and moon.
- Never bury the artwork under UI decoration. Product proof gets the cleanest frame.

### Typography

- Distressed or brush-like IchoTAKU logo only where the existing asset is supplied.
- Condensed uppercase display type for statements.
- Monospaced technical type for frequency, status, and metadata.
- Japanese text only when its meaning and typography are verified; do not use decorative pseudo-Japanese glyphs.

### Motion

- Signal appears as a scan, not a generic glow.
- Red marks interrupt; blue marks guide.
- Use quick hard cuts for archive fragments and slower dissolves for the bird.
- Product reveals should settle long enough to inspect the actual artwork.
- Respect reduced-motion delivery with a clean static version.

## Product reveal rules

The film can support many product families, but each must be shown honestly:

| Product | Film proof | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Digital tumbler wrap | Real tumbler mockup, flat wrap, straight/tapered proof, digital-only card | Showing a physical tumbler as if shipped |
| Physical T-shirt | Real garment mockup, fit/color/print placement | Calling it a digital download |
| Digital T-shirt art | Shirt preview plus isolated transparent artwork and file card | Pretending the shirt is included |
| Book/journal | Cover, interior pages, scale, physical/digital status | Showing a cover without clarifying format |
| Wall art | Framed scale plus actual printable ratio/file proof | Promising a physical print for a file listing |
| Sticker/label | Surface application, scale, finish/material | Tiny unreadable bundle collage |

Product scenes are placeholders until the exact Etsy listing and approved asset set are confirmed.

## Sound direction

```text
Music:      sparse midnight synth, low pulse, distant bowed texture
SFX:        radio scan, shortwave hiss, relay click, feather pass,
            city rain, one clean broadcast lock
VO:         optional; calm, close, lightly processed, never trailer-
            announcer loud
Silence:    hold 250–400 ms before the final CTA
```

The film must work without VO. Every essential message should exist as readable title text or be removed.

## Claims and approval gates

Before final edit, confirm:

- Every product shown exists in the shop or is explicitly labeled as a concept.
- “Digital download,” “commercial use,” “instant,” “official,” and format claims match the listing.
- No anime franchise or third-party character is presented as IchoTAKU-owned without documentation.
- Any sales, review, “best,” “original,” or popularity claim has a source and approval.
- The Etsy URL resolves and the end card uses the correct destination.
