# Product mockup guides

These guides are production profiles, not a requirement to fill every available Etsy image slot. Each image should answer a buyer question: what is it, how does it look in use, what is included, and what arrives after purchase?

## Universal rules

1. Use one clear hero image as the thumbnail.
2. Show the actual product or artwork before decorative lifestyle scenes.
3. Keep typography and important artwork readable at mobile thumbnail size.
4. Never put a physical-product mockup in a digital-download delivery slot.
5. State “digital download only” wherever a digital product could be mistaken for a physical item.
6. Use finished, flattened information cards for files, sizing, and disclaimers.
7. Keep one reusable profile per product family; change only the seasonal or colorway scene.

## 1. Digital tumbler wraps

**Buyer must understand:** the design, tumbler size, straight/tapered variants, and exact files delivered.

| Order | Mockup | Buyer question answered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Single clean tumbler hero | What does the design look like on a tumbler? |
| 2 | Full flat wrap | What artwork am I buying? |
| 3 | 180° or alternate angle | Does the design continue around the tumbler? |
| 4 | Straight vs. tapered comparison | Which file fits my tumbler? |
| 5 | Detail crop | Can I inspect the art quality? |
| 6 | One lifestyle scene | What is the mood or season? |
| 7 | Included-files card | What files are included? |
| 8 | Digital-only disclaimer | Is a physical tumbler shipped? |
| 9–10 | Bundle grids only | How many designs are included? |
| Video | 3–15 second rotation | How does the design wrap? |

**Delivery slots:** straight PNG, tapered PNG, instructions or ZIP, then bundle extras. Never place mockup images in delivery-file slots.

## 2. Physical POD T-shirts

**Buyer must understand:** fit, garment color, print placement, size, material, and fulfillment.

| Order | Mockup |
|---|---|
| 1 | Model wearing the shirt, front view |
| 2 | Clean isolated front view |
| 3 | Back view or alternate placement |
| 4 | Close-up of print texture |
| 5 | Available garment colors |
| 6 | Lifestyle/model scene |
| 7 | Size chart |
| 8 | Material and fit card |
| Video | Short model turn or fabric/detail clip |

**Do not use:** a digital-download disclaimer or transparent PNG as the primary image. This is a physical product and needs size, material, and shipping information.

## 3. Digital T-shirt designs

**Buyer must understand:** the artwork file, transparency, resolution, formats, license, and no physical shirt.

| Order | Mockup |
|---|---|
| 1 | One shirt mockup with large readable artwork |
| 2 | Isolated transparent artwork on a contrasting field |
| 3 | Close-up of print detail |
| 4 | Alternate shirt color |
| 5 | Front/back placement when included |
| 6 | File-format and resolution card |
| 7 | License/usage card |
| 8 | Digital-download-only card |
| Video | Optional print-placement or shirt-color sequence |

**Delivery slots:** PNG first; SVG, EPS, PSD, PDF, or ZIP only when actually included.

## 4. Books and journals

**Buyer must understand:** cover design, scale, interior type, page count, and whether the product is printable or physical.

### Physical book or journal

1. Front-cover hero at a readable angle.
2. Front and back cover view.
3. Interior spread or sample page.
4. Scale in hand or beside a familiar object.
5. Spine/binding detail.
6. Available sizes or editions.
7. Lifestyle reading or desk scene.
8. Paper, page count, and production details.

### Digital planner, journal, or printable book

1. Cover hero.
2. Interior spread showing real pages.
3. Several-page overview.
4. Printable-size comparison.
5. File-format and page-count card.
6. Device or printed-use example.
7. Download and license card.
8. Digital-only disclaimer.

**Do not lead with:** a tiny multi-page collage that makes every page unreadable.

## 5. Mugs and drinkware

1. Single product hero.
2. Opposite-side view.
3. Handle and print detail.
4. Scale or capacity reference.
5. Color/finish options.
6. Lifestyle use scene.
7. Care instructions.
8. Shipping or digital disclaimer, depending on product type.

For a digital mug-wrap file, use the tumbler logic: artwork proof first, then file inventory and digital disclaimer. Do not combine physical mug and digital wrap copy.

## 6. Posters, art prints, and wall art

1. Artwork hero with no competing text.
2. Framed wall-scale scene.
3. Unframed artwork view.
4. Close-up texture or detail.
5. Size comparison on a wall.
6. Available sizes and orientation.
7. Paper/print-quality card.
8. Digital-file disclaimer if printable.

For printable wall art, show the actual file ratio and a clean crop—not only a framed mockup.

## 7. Stickers, decals, and labels

1. Single sticker or label hero.
2. Sheet or bundle overview.
3. Scale in hand or on the intended surface.
4. Close-up of edge, finish, and texture.
5. Color/variant options.
6. Application scene.
7. Material and finish card.
8. Physical-versus-digital clarification.

Avoid a first image containing dozens of tiny stickers unless the bundle itself is the product and the individual designs remain readable.

## 8. Hoodies, sweatshirts, and totes

1. Front model view.
2. Clean isolated product view.
3. Back or alternate placement.
4. Close-up of print or embroidery.
5. Color options.
6. Fit, size, and material card.
7. Lifestyle scene.
8. Care instructions.

Keep apparel mockup profiles separate from digital artwork profiles. A Gildan hoodie mockup is never a delivery file for a PNG design.

## MyDesigns profile naming

Use stable names so CSV rows and folders remain predictable:

```text
Etsy Digital / Tumbler / Evergreen
Etsy Digital / Tumbler / Seasonal
Etsy Digital / Tumbler / Bundle
Etsy Physical / T-Shirt / Standard
Etsy Digital / T-Shirt / PNG-SVG
Etsy Physical / Book / Paperback
Etsy Digital / Book / Printable
Etsy Physical / Mug / Standard
Etsy Digital / Wall Art / Printable
Etsy Physical / Sticker / Sheet
Etsy Physical / Hoodie-Tote / Standard
```

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Hero represents the exact product being sold.
- [ ] Digital and physical product language is not mixed.
- [ ] Every promised file exists and is within the platform limit.
- [ ] Mockup product, size, and placement match the listing.
- [ ] The first image survives Etsy thumbnail cropping.
- [ ] The description states what is included and what is not.
- [ ] Seasonal changes are limited to the intended lifestyle slot.
- [ ] One draft is reviewed before bulk preparation.
