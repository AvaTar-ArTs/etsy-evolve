# Product listing generation notes

These CSVs are draft preparation artifacts for Etsy/MyDesigns. They do not publish listings, call an undocumented API, or claim that every referenced asset has been verified.

## Files

- `PRODUCT_LISTINGS_DRAFT.csv` — cross-family Etsy posting drafts.
- `MYDESIGNS_DRAFT_QUEUE.csv` — narrow MyDesigns preparation queue using the known Etsy Digital and Digital Bundles templates.

## Required review before upload

1. Replace placeholder delivery and mockup filenames with verified local assets.
2. Confirm whether each product is physical or digital; never mix those modes in one listing.
3. Confirm rights, licenses, file dimensions, included formats, and commercial-use language.
4. Keep personalization off for ordinary digital wraps and design files.
5. Use the tumbler slot order: hero, flat wrap, 180-degree view, straight/tapered comparison, close-up, then optional lifestyle/info cards.
6. Validate Etsy title, tag, file-count, and file-size limits in the live editor before upload.
7. Test one draft listing before any bulk operation.

## Deliberate safety choices

- Quantities are planning values, not inventory claims.
- Prices are starting hypotheses, not market guarantees.
- Trademarked franchise terms were not added to the drafts.
- Source directories are recorded for traceability; files were not copied or moved.
